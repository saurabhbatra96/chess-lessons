This is another Botvinnik game, this time in the Queen's Gambit Declined at the Soviet Championship of 1931, against Vsevolod Rauzer. Black looks passive for a while, but the pieces gradually find active squares, and when the position opens it is Black who is ready.

Compare this with the Lasker Defense earlier in the course. Whichever way you handle the opening, the Queen's Gambit Declined rewards patience. Black waits for White to commit, then gets the pieces into play.
