The pawns on e6 and d5 should feel familiar. This is the structure of your French, and a great many of the plans carry over. The drawback is the same as well: the bishop on c8 is hemmed in, and Black is short of space. The Lasker Defense answers that with exchanges. A jump to e4 forces two pairs of minor pieces off the board, and a cramped position needs room more than it needs anything else.
