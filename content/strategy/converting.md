The hardest position to win is a winning one. The opponent starts looking for tricks, you start relaxing, and a game that was over is suddenly not. Converting an advantage is a technique of its own, and it mostly consists of removing your opponent's chances.

## The Technique

- **Consolidate first.** Before you press on, make sure that all your pieces are protected and that your king is safe. A move that removes the last counter-chance is worth more than a move that gains another pawn.
- **Trade pieces.** Fewer pieces mean fewer tricks.
- **Create a passed pawn.** Passed pawns tie down defenders, and they end games.
- **Use forcing moves carefully.** Do not check for the sake of checking. Know what each one achieves.
- **Run the blunder check on every move.** This is the moment when it matters most, and the moment when you are most inclined to skip it.
