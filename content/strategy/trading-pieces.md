No exchange is neutral. Every trade removes something from the board, and the question is whether the thing removed was doing more for you or for your opponent.

## When to Trade

- **When you are ahead in material.** Each exchange makes your advantage proportionally larger. Trade pieces, not pawns, since the pawns are what you will need in order to win.
- **When you are short of space.** Cramped positions improve when pieces come off.
- **When your opponent's piece is better than yours.** Swap your bad bishop for their good one, or remove a knight from an outpost.
- **When a piece is defending something important.** Removing the defender leaves its charge alone.

## When Not to Trade

- When you are attacking. Your attackers are precious, and every piece that leaves the board helps the defender.
- When you have the space advantage. Your opponent wants exchanges, so make them work for them.
